# Surface brief: mobile app (Set Hub + Study Loop)

Scope: full mobile IA rebuild under mobile/. Mode: Operate.
Audience/job/action/proof/constraints: per locked shape brief —
solo studier resumes study in seconds; citations from existing sources
field; readiness always legible; no new backend; server/ and web/ untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One pool of lamplight on a dark desk. The app refuses the
category-default indigo dashboard of metric cards and eyebrow kickers;
focus is a lit surface, everything else recedes into ink. Study actions
live inside the light.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink grounds, warm lamp-pool surfaces, paper content
panels; one amber accent owns primary actions and active marks only.
Status reads as hairline bands, never filled pills; text stays
achromatic. System sans at a tight fixed scale; radii 12-16; elevation
is border or shadow, never both. No kickers, no emoji icons, no nested
cards.

STORY: The studier opens to their lit desk — the current set, its
readiness, one Continue action. They ask, summarize, or practice from a
sticky bar; every generated claim sits beside its source chip; done
items keep a visited trace in the feed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Study home. Top: set title + readiness hairline.
Center: the Continue panel in the pool with the single primary action.
Below: capture entry and recent activity rows. Primary action sits
thumb-high, full-width, amber fill.

FORM: Lamp-pool carrel, candidate 3 of 7 grounded (index-card box,
margins, lamp pool, whiteboard, planner, lab notebook, cloth binding);
seed key 9bcf030d. Raises carried: proof pairing, hairline status,
literal labeling, single grid, REVEAL-first, heavier active + history
traces. Challenger verdicts: competitive — mono proof-pair, cloud
fringe, teletext REVEAL; declined — club scrawl, streetwear quotes,
ascii phosphor. Canon standard offered and declined.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with
the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster
carrying its provenance
