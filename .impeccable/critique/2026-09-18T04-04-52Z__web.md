## Overall Impression

The trust writing is excellent and the proof language (citation chips everywhere) is genuinely own-material. But the single action the whole site exists to produce — install the app — is broken, and the two largest visuals sell infrastructure instead of studying. Fix the CTA truth and the hero, and this jumps a full rating band.

## What's Working

1. **Citation chips as a visual language.** The same bordered chip (`lecture-notes.pdf · p. 4`) recurs in the mock, all four tool rows, and trust copy — positioning made scannable.
2. **Google-scope candor.** Home lede, three cards, FAQ, and Privacy state the same three shared items and the same never-list, down to the permissions URL. Redundancy as trust engineering.
3. **A deletion page that respects the user it is losing.** Alternatives before destruction, exact consequences, 30-day backup timeline, typed DELETE confirmation, kind success copy.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] Every "Google Play" CTA links to the store homepage, not the app.** Header, hero badge, mobile menu, footer (`SiteHeader.tsx:82,156`, `Home.tsx:245`, `SiteFooter.tsx:37`). The primary action cannot succeed. Why it matters: the highest-traffic click on the site is a misdirection. Fix: point at the real listing; until it exists, ship one honest state (coming-soon/waitlist anchor) and drop the store-badge styling. Suggested command: $impeccable clarify
- **[P1] Hero sells infrastructure; the product mock is dead code.** `Home.tsx:289-328` renders the arch diagram + fullscreen modal; `ProductVisual.tsx` is never imported. Why it matters: first-timers bounce on jargon before seeing a study set. Fix: render `<ProductVisual/>` as the hero figure, demote the diagram to the Architecture section, delete the hero modal (~45 lines). Suggested command: $impeccable layout
- **[P1] Architecture band overwhelms and breaks its own palette.** Six flow cells + six rainbow-dot layer cards + protocol footnote, with pink/amber/purple/teal accents against DESIGN.md's one-blue/one-green rule. Why it matters: ~6 viewports of engineer tax on the student path; a documented system contradicting itself. Fix: keep the 6-step flow + pipeline box, fold layer cards into one "full stack" details element, recolor dots to brand/moss. Suggested command: $impeccable distill
- **[P1] Deletion depends on mailto: with no fallback.** `DeleteAccount.tsx:43` sets success state regardless of whether a mail client opened. Why it matters: phones without configured mail show "Request composed" for an unsent request. Fix: copyable prefilled body + Copy button stated before the mailto attempt. Suggested command: $impeccable harden
- **[P2] Routing lies about location.** Unknown hashes render home silently; section jumps never write the hash; privacy index fakes links. Why it matters: no bookmarking, sharing, or history integrity. Fix: real hash section URLs and a minimal 404 for unknown routes. Suggested command: $impeccable harden
